## What MindPulse does

MindPulse listens for room-level sound patterns on your device to help
notice possible baby disturbances. It is **not a medical device and not a
certified baby monitor**, does not detect breathing, apnea, or medical
emergencies, and should not be relied on as your sole means of checking on
your baby.

## What we collect

**On your device only (never leaves your device unless you explicitly
choose to sign in):**
- Live microphone audio, analyzed in real time to detect sound patterns.
  Raw audio is never stored, streamed, or uploaded.
- Optional parent-recorded lullaby clips, stored only locally on your
  device.
- Local learning preferences and sleep history, stored on-device via
  browser local storage.

**If you create an account (optional):**
- Your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your
  password in plain text).
- Your baby's name and birth month, if you add a baby profile.
- Derived sleep session data: start/end times, a computed sleep quality
  score, and event types (e.g., "restless," "wake") — never raw audio.
- If you opt in to "Help improve detection" in Settings: derived numeric
  features (e.g., relative activity level) tied to a sleep event, used to
  improve detection. Raw audio is never included in this.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use your data for advertising or share it with advertisers.
- We do not upload raw audio recordings under any circumstance.
